# Context Help Guide

1. Hover over any interactive control to display its purpose and safety behavior.
2. Click the `?` button in the top title bar or press `F1` to activate Help Mode.
3. While Help Mode is active, click any button, field, grid, tab, list, or setting to open its full instructions.
4. Commands are not executed while Help Mode is active.
5. Press `F1` again or click `? ON` to leave Help Mode.
6. Press `Shift+F1` to open the full user guide.
