# Part 134 test guide

1. Open a site and enter Suggested Changes.
2. Press Generate from audits. Confirm that the full-window loader appears and Ribbon/site navigation cannot be clicked.
3. Confirm the progress text moves through audit findings, proposals, and automation policy.
4. Select a directly executable low-risk proposal and choose Apply now.
5. Confirm the loader shows evidence, WordPress write, and verification stages.
6. Confirm the overlay closes after success or failure and the active site remains unchanged.
